Why we exist

Our revised strategic framework reflects our focus on Homabay County.

Vision

An empowered, inclusive and climate-resilient community where every person, regardless of gender, age or background, has equal opportunities and resources to thrive in a sustainable environment.

Mission

To empower youth and women in Homabay County through reproductive health education, economic empowerment, climate-smart livelihoods, advocacy and legal rights awareness, so they can define and transcend their own limits.

Strategic pillars

Three core pillars for community transformation.

Climate Change & Smart Agriculture

Economic empowerment is incorporated here, promoting climate-smart livelihoods for sustainable futures.

Health & Well-being

A broader, holistic approach to community health, including reproductive health education.

Community Advocacy

Legal rights are incorporated here, empowering communities through awareness and advocacy.
